## What Was Implemented

- Django app `infrastructure.persistence.catalogue` (`CatalogueConfig`, label `catalogue`), registered in `INSTALLED_APPS`
- `GradeModel` (`catalogue_grade`) and `SubjectModel` (`catalogue_subject`) with paired `_ro`/`_en` names (ADR-006), PROTECT FK, and the two named unique constraints
- `0001_initial` (schema, generated by `makemigrations`)
- `0002_seed_catalogue`: reversible `RunPython` with the data inlined, using the owner-confirmed content:
  - **10 levels** in display order: K = Grădiniță, P = Clasa pregătitoare, then grades 1–8
  - **111 subjects**: K has the 5 experiential domains, P–8 use the confirmed lists
  - Only grade 2 CLR, MEM and EN are active
  - Forward uses `get_or_create` (idempotent in effect). Reverse deletes exactly the seeded pairs and fails loudly through PROTECT.
- `tests/factories/catalogue.py`: `GradeFactory` and `SubjectFactory` (`django_get_or_create` on codes)
- 46 new integration tests across 5 files, including subprocess tests of real `manage.py migrate` runs

## Deviations from Plan

- **Owner-requested data change (AC10):** 10 levels instead of 9. Grades 1–8 now have `sort_order` 2–9, not the story's 1–8, so that `P` fits between K and 1. Kindergarten holds 5 domains, and the old K list moved to the new `P`. The story file (`docs/plans/**`) is the coordinator's to update; I didn't edit it.
- **`unseed` deletes by seeded `(grade, code)` pairs, not by `grade__code__in`.** The step excerpt would also delete subjects added later to a seeded grade, which contradicts AC5 ("Rows added later by other means are untouched"). With pair-scoped deletion, such a subject is kept and its grade's deletion raises `ProtectedError`. That's the "fail loudly rather than orphan" behaviour, and it's tested.
- `__str__` added to both models (ruff `DJ008`); `ClassVar` annotations on `Meta.ordering`/`constraints` and on the hand-written migration's class attributes (ruff `RUF012`). There's no schema effect (`makemigrations --check` clean).
- Extra tests beyond the story's list: the full content comparison, the kindergarten domains, schema introspection, `migrate` subprocess runs, seed re-run, unseed scope, and factories.
- **Environment limitation (not used by this story):** GNU gettext/`msgfmt` isn't installed. This story adds no UI strings, so no `.po` change was needed. Story 1.4 installs gettext.

## Lessons Learned

1. A step's code excerpt can contradict its own AC (unseed scope). Test the AC wording, not just the excerpt.
2. To test PROTECT behaviour against future tables, register a probe model under an installed app label, or Django won't see the reverse relation.
3. Recompute derived counts from the data rather than adding deltas: moving a list between levels isn't an addition (111, not 119).
